:How to Install Orbit Menu (Free Version)

1-Transfer EBOOT.BIN folder to path "/dev_hdd0/game/(Your GTA Title ID)/USRDIR" on your PS3
2-Transfer "Orbit" folder to path "/dev_hdd0/tmp" on your PS3 (This folder must be replaced in every update)
3-Transfer your "Orbit.sprx" to path "/dev_hdd0/tmp" on your PS3
4-Transfer your "orbit.key" to path "/dev_hdd0/tmp" on your PS3



#You can Transfer files by using Filzilla on PC with Multiman on PS3 or Rebug toolbox

(FOR CEX & HEN USERS)
1-Make sure you have WEBMAN installed on you PS3 to use the Orbit Menu
2-if you use HEN firmware you should know what type of your Game PSN or Backup.
: PSN game is installed by PKG file and the game appears on a XMB screen.
: Backup Game is copied game and you can startup the game from Multiman or Webman.


How to Open:
Hold on (L1 + X)
