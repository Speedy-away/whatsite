HDDChecker

Inspect internal HDD identity and S.M.A.R.T. status, scan surfaces and check APA/PFS structures. This is an internal-drive diagnostic tool.

  • Internal HDD
  • S.M.A.R.T.
  • APA / PFS
  • Diagnostics
Requires
An ATA-compatible internal PS2 HDD connection. Upstream specifies ATA-4, UDMA 4, S.M.A.R.T. and drives up to 2 TB; USB storage is not the target disk.

Setup & verified files

Extract the complete release and launch its ELF from your homebrew launcher. Begin with drive information. Back up saves and files before repairs: remapping writes to bad sectors, and zero-fill erases the disk. Keep generated scan logs with your backup.

  • HDD

  • ELF
Requires
PS2 with an internal HDD interface, a drive and an ELF launcher. These benchmarks do not measure USB or SMB and are not a promise of game loading speed.

Requires
A PS2 Ethernet interface, a wired network, an ELF boot method and a compatible PC development client such as ps2client. This download is the PS2 default-loading build only.
