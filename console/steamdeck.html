Worth Knowing

No Exploit Needed

SteamOS is Linux and Valve does not lock it down. Installing plugins is supported behaviour, not a jailbreak, and it will not get you banned.

Desktop Mode

Hold the power button and pick Desktop Mode to get a normal KDE desktop. Most installs are easier from there.

SteamOS Updates

A SteamOS update can wipe things written outside your home folder. Decky reinstalls itself, but check your plugins after a big update.

Bring Your Own Games

EmuDeck installs emulators, not games. Supply your own dumps and BIOS files from hardware you own.
