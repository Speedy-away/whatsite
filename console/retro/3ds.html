Choose CIA, 3DSX or FIRM

  1. Prepare storageUse a genuine SD / microSD with FAT32 and an MBR partition table. The maintained guide recommends 2–128 GB; larger cards can introduce GBA graphics and custom-theme issues. Keep the entire existing Nintendo 3DS folder and your saves backed up.
  2. CIA apps: install to HOME MenuCopy a CIA to a convenient SD folder such as /cias/, then use FBI on your prepared CFW system to install it. Read the app requirements first. Universal-Updater is another route for supported apps.
  3. 3DSX apps: launch through Homebrew LauncherPut the executable in /3ds/ or its documented app subfolder. Copy bundled data directories separately to their specified SD paths. A lone 3DSX is not a replacement for required game data.
  4. FIRM files: boot payloadsFor tools that document it, put the FIRM under /luma/payloads/ and use the Luma chainloader at boot. Luma boot.firm itself belongs at the SD root; do not replace it with an app FIRM.
  5. DS mode: use the 3DS packageFollow TWiLight Menu++’s 3DS installation page. The DS flashcart and DSi packages use different instructions.
