Brawl · console setup

  1. Check the discThe packages target NTSC-U Super Smash Bros. Brawl, RSBE01. Do not rename a PAL/Japanese game ID to make it look compatible.
  2. Prepare a separate SD cardExtract the selected console package. For Project+, open its outer Project+ v3.2.1 (Wii Lite) folder and copy the apps, Project+ and private folders to SD root. For Minus, open Brawl Minus v5.1.5 Console and copy its contents to SD root. Keep each launcher and its mod data together. Project+ Lite is the full Wii gameplay release with compressed music for a 2 GB SD card; allow space beyond the ZIP size.
  3. Use the included launcherWith Homebrew Channel already working, launch that mod’s supplied app with the matching disc inserted. Follow its packaged instructions for its disc route. Hackless and USB methods have different storage requirements; use the linked official guide for those routes.
  4. Update as a complete packageKeep the previous SD contents backed up. Brawl Minus 5.1.5 requires a fresh download; the creator says it cannot auto-update from 5.1. Do not combine files from Project+, Minus or older releases.
