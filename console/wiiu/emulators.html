Choose the correct Wii U format

FormatInstallation
WUHBWii U Homebrew Bundle for supported Aroma home-menu launching. It can contain its executable and app resources in one file. Put it under sd:/wiiu/apps/ at the documented path.
RPX / ELFWii U executables. Some RPX builds support Aroma; others and legacy ELF homebrew need Tiramisu/Homebrew Launcher. Check the specific build, rather than assuming every RPX is compatible.
WPS / WMSPlugins and modules, not app-menu executables. Preserve the complete package layout under /wiiu/environments/aroma/plugins/ and modules/. Inkay’s bundle here includes both.
SD ZIPExtract the required app files to their documented folders. Most store bundles mirror the SD-root layout; FTPiiU’s official release ZIP is flat, so its WPS must be copied into the plugin folder explicitly.
Wii / vWii DOLRuns in the separate Wii environment. Follow the vWii guide before adding those apps; continue to the Wii resources for Wii-format tools.
Source / PC helperSource archives require building, and desktop/mobile binaries run on another device. The downloads here are Wii U console builds, not a PC Wii U emulator.
