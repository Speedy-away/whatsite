Choose the right console environment

These are complete, checked distribution packages. Native Wii U builds and vWii builds are separate downloads. Keep the full folder layout and all music/data assets. Commercial base games, updates and DLC remain user-supplied. Large original mod archives use their publisher’s download service and have preserved copies in the local F: archive.

Back up saves and SD contents before adding a mod. Use its original release instructions for exact game, region and revision support. An archive integrity check is not a physical-console compatibility test.
