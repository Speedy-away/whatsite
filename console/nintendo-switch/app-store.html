Install on your microSD card

  1. Start with working homebrewYour original Switch must already be able to launch hbmenu. Follow your model-specific setup guide first.
  2. Choose the complete bundle or NROExtract the complete ZIP to the microSD root with its switch/appstore/ folder intact, or copy appstore.nro into sd:/switch/appstore/. Keep your existing apps and configuration.
  3. Launch the storeSafely eject the card, boot your CFW setup, and open Homebrew App Store from hbmenu. Prefer full application mode. Internet access is needed to browse and download apps.
  4. Install an app and read its requirementsUse the console store interface to choose an app and inspect its notes. A store download does not guarantee compatibility with every system version.
