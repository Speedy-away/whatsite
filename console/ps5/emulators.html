Installing console emulator ports

  1. Read the release requirementsConfirm your firmware, homebrew enabler, loader and helper payloads match the project release.
  2. Install the correct console buildFollow the project’s homebrew-folder, PKG or ELF-installer instructions. A PC executable cannot be copied over as a PS5 app.
  3. Add your game filesUse your own dumps and required BIOS or firmware. Follow the emulator’s documented directory layout.
  4. Test one titleCheck controller input, audio and saves before expanding your library. Report problems to the console-port project.
