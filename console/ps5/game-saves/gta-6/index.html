Pair your PS5

  1. Install GarlicTransfer. Download version 0.3.12 above and install it on your Windows PC. Connect your PC and PS5 to the same local network.
  2. Discover your console. In GarlicTransfer, click Discover, select your PS5 from the list, then click Pair this PS5.
  3. Select the pairing entry on your PS5. Go to Settings → System → System Software → Data Transfer and select GarlicTransfer Pairing when it appears.
  4. Wait for pairing to finish. During pairing, your PS5 may show “Can’t transfer data.” Leave that screen open and wait for GarlicTransfer to confirm pairing. Its pairing window closes automatically when pairing succeeds.

Prepare your save upload

  1. Prepare the upload. In GarlicTransfer, go to Upload and click Prepare Upload. Select the PS5 user whose PSN account you resigned the save to.
  2. Add the save folder. Extract the ZIP, then click Add Save Folder and select the folder containing your resigned save.
  3. Enter the title ID. Under Game Title ID for External Saves, enter PPSA01547. You can browse PS5 title IDs in PlayStation-Titles .

Upload your save

  1. Start the upload. Click Upload Saves and follow the on-screen instructions. Choose User Data when asked which data type to transfer.
  2. Wait for the upload to finish. Once the transfer completes, open GTA 6 on the PS5 account you selected and check that the save loads.

Account reminder. The save must be resigned to your own PSN account before it will work with your user profile.
