Queue records and automatic retries belong to ezRemote. Use its native file manager to inspect a failed transfer or partial file; this page does not delete files or send automatic retry requests.

Ready files do not need archive extraction. PKGs still need installation, payloads need a compatible loader, and plugins, cheats or patches need their documented setup and supporting files. Original and prepared archives remain available under Archive files. Their extraction links open the running client’s preparation page; reviewed archives up to 32 MiB receive a checksum check before extraction into a new folder.

PS5 websrv must also be running on port 8080 for destination-folder checks and storage detection. A listing confirms existence, not free space or write permission. External-device mounts are rechecked for each save. “Receiver reports complete” reflects the receiver’s status. It does not verify the saved file’s hash or console compatibility.
