Install a PS4 emulator port

  1. Enable homebrew firstCheck the project’s firmware requirements against your actual console and HEN version.
  2. Install its PS4 packageDownload the correct console release and follow its PKG installer instructions. Keep PC and PS4 builds separate.
  3. Add required firmware and your gamesUse the directories specified by the project. For the RPCS3 port, obtain PS3UPDAT.PUP from PlayStation and install it through that port’s firmware menu.
  4. Test and keep saves backed upTry a single title and verify controller input, sound and saves. Use the port’s issue tracker for support.
