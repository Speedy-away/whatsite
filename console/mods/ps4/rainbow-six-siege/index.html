  1. Copy the SPRXSave the file as /data/NIGGERWARE/r6s.sprx.
  2. Update the plugin configurationOpen /data/GoldHEN/plugins.ini. Merge the entry for your installed title ID, CUSA01788 or CUSA-01800. Preserve existing plugin entries.
  3. Enable plugins and launch the gameIn GoldHEN, open Plugin Settings and turn on Enable Plugins. Launch Rainbow Six Siege.
