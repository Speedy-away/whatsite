Add an app to the Homebrew Channel

  1. Extract the console buildFor an app ZIP with an apps folder, copy that folder to the root of the storage device used by your Wii setup. Keep each app in its own subfolder.
  2. Keep the executable and app information togetherA normal Homebrew Channel app folder contains boot.dol or boot.elf, with meta.xml and icon.png when supplied.
  3. Set up Nintendont’s three filesRename the downloaded loader.dol to boot.dol and place it with its linked meta.xml and icon.png in /apps/Nintendont/. This page pins the upstream build snapshot rather than assigning it a new release number.
  4. Launch the app and finish its own setupOpen the Homebrew Channel, select the app and follow its project instructions. Loaders, storage devices and disc tools can have additional requirements.
