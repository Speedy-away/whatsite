Peer Pressure is a persistent Xbox 360 softmod for Xenon, Zephyr, Falcon, Jasper and Trinity motherboards. Corona and Winchester are unsupported. It requires an attached HDD; the developer recommends a mechanical drive. Read its installation wiki before changing NAND or HDD data. DashLaunch and DashLaunch plugins are not supported.
