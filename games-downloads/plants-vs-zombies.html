About Fusion Mods

Community Made

Fusion mods are fan projects with no connection to PopCap or EA. They are not official releases.

Scan Your Downloads

Fan mods get re-uploaded a lot. Stick to the original creator's release and scan anything before running it.

Back Up Saves

Install a mod to a copy of the game, not your main install, so your original saves stay intact.

Translation Varies

Several fusion builds started out in Chinese. English patches exist but coverage is patchy.
